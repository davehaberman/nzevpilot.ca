He’s a Conservative!

Eye-watering diesel prices helped attract over 100 fleet operators and heavy duty truck leaders to the Save-On-Foods Memorial Arena on Friday, November 13th to see a BYD Class 8 semi and a Type A Shuttle Bus. The Victoria EV Association organized the event. MLA Bert Chen, (centre) for the Cowichan Valley stopped by for a test ride and chat with attendees. He was the only MLA to show up and made quite an impression!

Dave Haberman

BYD Launches in Canada

BYD has just launched their Canadian website, announcing they're "coming soon" — visit byd.com/en-ca.

Our BYD Shark 6 will be on display at Woodgrove Mall in Nanaimo this Sunday, September 6, at the 2nd Annual Cruise-In Car Show. Admission is free. Stop by to see one of Canada's first "extended range" EV trucks.

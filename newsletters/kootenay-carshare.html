Kootenay Carshare

Carsharing is a fantastic transportation concept. I drove my BYD, with a Neighbourhood EV in the box, to Nelson for a meeting with George and the Kootenay Carshare board.

Founded 25 years ago, Kootenay Carshare is the oldest and most successful co-operative of its kind — today it has 550 members.

George's read: we can build the same thing for Vancouver Island and the Gulf Islands, but with an all-EV fleet.

Big thanks to George and the whole Kootenay Carshare board for the warm welcome.

For more details visit nzevpilot.ca.

Cheers, Dave Haberman

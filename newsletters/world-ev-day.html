World EV Day

World EV Day. Since 2020, World EV Day has united governments, automakers, and everyday drivers from over one hundred countries, all celebrating the shift to electric mobility.

This year Canada's single appearance was the Plug'N Drive Toronto Meetup representing EV drivers nationwide. We're calling on our local mayors, MLAs, First Nation partners, federal government, and NGOs to join us in putting Vancouver Island on the map for World EV Day 2027.

Cheers, Dave Haberman

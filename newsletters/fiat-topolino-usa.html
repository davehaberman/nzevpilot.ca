Fiat Topolino Arrives in the USA

This EU built car is now on sale across the US as a street-legal Neighbourhood EV priced at $14,985 delivered. It comes as a standard 2 door hardtop or as the Dolce Vita, with a roll-off soft top and open air sides.

With rising fuel prices and higher cost of living weighing on American households, the Topolino is a natural fit for families needing a second car for town, as well as seniors and new drivers.

The US and Mexico are getting these low-cost electric vehicles, but Canada isn't. We invite BC's new leaders to consider a Vancouver Island pilot.

Cheers,

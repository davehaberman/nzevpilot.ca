Light'EmUp TV Cruise-In

Light'EmUp TV, Vancouver Island's largest car show organization, is back with another event. Michael Nicholson, a Victoria-based automotive media creator/influencer, is hosting the 2nd Annual Cruise-In Car Show this Sunday at the Nanaimo Woodgrove Mall, 11am–4pm.

Our BYD truck/NEV car will be there. Admission is free — stop by to see what we're up to.

Cheers, Dave Haberman
